A reading list on stochastic quantisation

  1. Paracontrolled distributions and singular PDEs
    Massimiliano Gubinelli, Peter Imkeller and Nicolas Perkowski
    2012 · in Papers of Massimiliano Gubinelli · vetted: version 5 · landing page · PDF · pin 07db5eb2e13e617d1bcdca6d1cad2ebf…
    Abstract

    We introduce an approach to study certain singular PDEs which is based on techniques from paradifferential calculus and on ideas from the theory of controlled rough paths. We illustrate its applicability on some model problems like differential equations driven by fractional Brownian motion, a fractional Burgers type SPDE driven by space-time white noise, and a non-linear version of the parabolic Anderson model with a white noise potential.

  2. A variational method for $\Phi^4_3$
    N. Barashkov and M. Gubinelli
    2018 · in Papers of Massimiliano Gubinelli · vetted: version 2 · landing page · PDF · pin d3d6f8e6722b4f663b545e3433ce030f…
    Abstract

    We introduce an explicit description of the $\Phi^4_3$ measure on a bounded domain. Our starting point is the interpretation of its Laplace transform as the value function of a stochastic optimal control problem along the flow of a scale regularization parameter. Once small scale singularities have been renormalized by the standard counterterms, $\Gamma$-convergence allows to extend the variational characterization to the unregularized model.

  3. Mass gap for the fractional $\Phi^4_2$ model
    Paweł Duch and Massimiliano Gubinelli
    2026 · in Papers of Massimiliano Gubinelli · vetted: version 1 · landing page · PDF · pin 6c761128c8ccee9dedf88e0e04566e6b…
    Abstract

    We present a new, simple and self-contained technique for proving a mass gap for Euclidean quantum field theories in the high-temperature regime using the stochastic quantisation approach. As an application, we establish exponential decay of spatial correlations for the fractional $\Phi^{4}$ model on the plane in the Wick-ordering regime. The key idea is to analyse the propagation of perturbations using the linearised stochastic quantisation equation. Spatial propagation is controlled by an auxiliary random walk, while the potentially destabilising effect of the renormalised nonlinearity is controlled by combining dissipative energy estimates for the nonlinear dynamic with the Boué–Dupuis variational formula.

  6. A singular integration by parts formula for the exponential Euclidean QFT on the plane
    Francesco C. De Vecchi, Massimiliano Gubinelli and Mattia Turra
    2022 · vetted: version 1 · PDF · pin 617ec0bd9cc57510ccd8291a542261e5…
    Abstract

    We give a novel characterization of the Euclidean quantum field theory with exponential interaction $\nu$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$ through a renormalized integration by parts (IbP) formula, or otherwise said via an Euclidean Dyson-Schwinger equation for expected values of observables. In order to obtain the well-posedness of the singular IbP problem, we import some ideas used to analyse singular SPDEs and we require the measure to "look like" the Gaussian free field (GFF) in the sense that a suitable Wasserstein distance from the GFF is finite. This guarantees the existence of a nice coupling with the GFF which allows to control the renormalized IbP formula.
